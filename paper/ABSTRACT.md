# Extended abstract (UNPUBLISHED; manuscript in revision)

I work out a multi-qubit architecture for interlayer excitons in a gate-defined trap lattice on an aligned TMD heterobilayer. The qubit is the position of a single dark IX in a double trap (dual rail), all logic is electrical, and the two-qubit gate is a polarize-and-phase CZ protected by a mid-gate polarity-flip echo. Loss, the dominant failure, is converted into a flagged erasure by a photon-free electrical occupancy probe.

Three budgets survive internal review: (i) a double-trap map shows a wide OFF-state tunnelling window (t ≤ 0.05 µeV) with a 10–213 ps barrier-gate X gate and < 10⁻³ leakage; (ii) image-charge sums show that a single bottom gate does not screen the vertical-dipole coupling (4.1–6.8 µeV for 5–20 nm gate distances), while a dual-gate sandwich suppresses beyond-neighbour crosstalk exponentially; (iii) the echo cancels quasi-static site-energy disorder exactly, reducing the 10⁻³ gate target to a single ns-correlated noise-floor specification (≲ 0.4 µeV).

Open items under revision: trap geometry below gate-stack resolution, one trim line per trap (no crossbar), the threshold-margin comparison, and contact-based reset. Per-site control layers are fatally limited by disorder at the system level (see github.com/FarukSamiBilgin/moire-300-logical-qubit-feasibility).
